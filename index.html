Common questions

What is included in the free mini-audit?

A quick review of your Google Business Profile, local search visibility, reviews and basic website SEO, with the top fixes that will help you get more calls.

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Small profile and review fixes can show movement in a few weeks. Stronger rankings in competitive areas usually take 3 to 6 months of steady work.

Can you remove negative Google reviews?

Reviews that break Google's policies (fake, spam, off-topic or abusive) can be reported and flagged properly. Genuine reviews cannot be removed, so for those I help you respond well and build more positive reviews.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangladesh?
